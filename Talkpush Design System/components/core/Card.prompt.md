The base surface for every panel, table, and callout. White fill, hairline border, 8-14px radius (10px default), soft shadow.

```jsx
<Card accent="var(--color-green)">Body content</Card>
```

`accent` draws a 4px top border in a brand color (rarely needed; most cards are plain). `hover` raises the shadow on mouse-over, for clickable cards only.
